## Introduction

If you have already gone through some of the articles published on the blog, you have been able to realize the opinion I have about sharing knowledge with other people. Today I want to tell you a little about another way in which we are transferring and receiving knowledge, a new community created to learn, share, enjoy and support each other, its name is _Python Tuluá_.

And yes, I'm going to say it even though it seems a little obvious. In this group we love the Python programming language and everything that has to do with its community, good practices, _frameworks,_ libraries, among other aspects. The best thing is that you don't have to be an expert to belong to our community, all you need is the desire and willingness you have to learn and share your knowledge with everyone.

## Some history

Credit must be given to [Isabel Cristina Ruíz](https://github.com/iris9112), a new friend and colleague; was the one who thought of creating a community of python enthusiasts in the city of Tuluá, then more friends and acquaintances joined us. But really the idea was born from her. He also accompanies us in the _meetups_.

## What do we do?

We hold meetings (_meetups_) once a month in a talk/practice mode, but we are also going to implement workshops, videos and innovate as ideas arise. We bring guests from other communities and cities to share their knowledge with the Tulueña community. The common thing about _meetups_ is that two talks are held: one advanced and one for beginners. So we have themes for all tastes and levels.

Come join Python Tuluá, bring your friends and start learning with us, it's very cool to learn with other people, surround yourself with people who pursue your same goals.
