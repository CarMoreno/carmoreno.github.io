For a long time I had been thinking about creating a blog to share my experiences and what I have learned throughout these years (a little over 3 years, if my memory doesn't fail me) in this programming world. And today I have finally decided to do it. You always think you can't contribute much to this community, but every little bit counts. That's why I decided to create this blog.

What will I talk about? I'm a huge fan of web programming, and I love Python, it's a very cool language, although I also like Java and recently PHP. This blog will try to cover topics related to programming in general, design patterns, good programming practices, some tips to be more productive in Python (and other languages) and the occasional off-topic, because we are programmers but we are also people, right? The posts won't have a predefined sequence, I will upload as I can, maybe often, sometimes not so much, although now that I've started writing I'm quite excited.

In the next post I will talk about how I created this site, but in advance I give my thanks to [Jacob Tomlinson][1], the creator of this dark theme I chose for my blog.
