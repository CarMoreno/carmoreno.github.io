# Introduction
New year, many objectives to meet and many goals to achieve, we have 365 days to get the most out of them and make the most of them. I write this post to express my resolutions for this new year, it is a good idea to have your resolutions written down, this will help you not lose your way as the days and months go by.

My list is quite extensive, and obviously it will be a challenge to complete them all, but you know what they say: _"It is better to throw the stone high"_. At the end of the year, I will return to this post to review the objectives that I achieved and those that I did not, and I will take stock of it (I hope that more objectives are achieved).
